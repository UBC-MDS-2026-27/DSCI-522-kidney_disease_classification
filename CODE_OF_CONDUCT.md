# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to creating a welcoming, inclusive, safe and respectful environment for everyone involved in the Kidney Disease Classification project.

We welcome contributions from people of different backgrounds, experiences, and skill levels, regardless of age, gender, race, ethnicity, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or other personal characteristics.

All participants are expected to treat others fairly and respectfully.

## Expected Behavior

We encourage all contributors to:

- Be respectful, professional, and considerate when communicating with others.
- Welcome different perspectives, experiences, and ideas.
- Provide constructive feedback and accept criticism respectfully.
- Respect others' time, effort, and contributions.
- Work collaboratively and take responsibility for their contributions.
- Use inclusive and welcoming language.
- Resolve disagreements through open and respectful communication.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or bullying.
- Offensive or discriminatory language, comments, or jokes.
- Personal attacks, insults, or disrespectful criticism.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions or project activities.
- Any other behavior that creates an unsafe or unwelcoming environment.
- Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behavior is expected to comply immediately.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project contributors and participants, including interactions through GitHub issues, pull requests, code reviews, project discussions, and other communication channels related to this project.

## Reporting and Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report the incident privately to a project maintainer through an agreed team contact channel.

When reporting an incident, please provide relevant details, including what happened, when it occurred, and any supporting information.

We encourage resolving concerns internally within the team first, whenever appropriate and safe to do so.

The following steps will be taken:

1.  **Internal discussion**: Team members will discuss the issue respectfully and work together to find a fair resolution.
2.  **Review and resolution**: The team will review the situation, consider different perspectives, and agree on appropriate actions while maintaining confidentiality to the extent reasonably possible.
3.  **Escalation**: If the issue cannot be resolved internally, the team will seek further guidance and assistance from the DSCI 522 instructor and TA.

For serious incidents, where internal discussion is inappropriate or unsafe, individuals may contact the DSCI 522 teaching team directly without first attempting internal resolution.

## Consequences of Unacceptable Behavior

Depending on the severity of the incident, consequences may include:

- A verbal or written warning.
- A request to stop or correct the behavior.
- Temporary restrictions on project participation.
- Rejection of contributions or removal of repository access, where permitted.
- Escalation to the DSCI 522 instructor and TA for further action.

Serious or repeated violations may result in stronger consequences.
